> Translated from Luyện-tập (Vietnamese) for BK Study Desk 1.1.4.

Take quizzes, write your own questions, ask AI to write them and send them to friends. No programming needed: you only select, type, copy and paste.

The Practice view is in Vietnamese only, so this page shows each Vietnamese label in **bold** with its meaning in parentheses. The screenshots show the Vietnamese interface.

## 1. Open Practice

Select **Practice** on the top navigation bar.

![Practice page](images/1-luyen-tap.png)

The page has:
- **Ôn hôm nay** (Review today): questions the app wants you to redo. A question you get wrong comes back the next day; one you get right comes back later and later.
- **Thi thử đề ngẫu nhiên** (Random mock exam): the app picks questions into a timed exam.
- **Môn học** (Subjects): select a subject to start.
- **Quiz tự soạn và đã nhập** (Your and imported quizzes): quizzes you wrote or got from friends. To remove one, right-click it.

## 2. Take a quiz

1. Select a subject, for example *Giải tích 1* (Calculus 1).
2. Select a lesson, for example *Đạo hàm* (Derivatives).

   ![Lessons in a subject](images/2-mon.png)

3. Read the **Kiến thức** (Key points) section at the top, then select an answer.
4. The app tells you **Đúng** (Correct) or **Sai** (Wrong) and shows the **lời giải** (solution) right below.

![Answering a question and seeing the solution](images/3-lam-bai.png)

Each question has two buttons:
- **Ghi chú** (Note): write down what to remember.
- **Nghi đáp án sai** (Answer may be wrong): flag a question whose answer looks wrong.

These questions are collected in the **Đánh dấu** (Flagged) box on the Practice page.

> Tip: to shuffle the questions or the answer options, select **Xáo câu** (Shuffle questions) or **Xáo đáp án** (Shuffle answers) at the top right.

## 3. Write your own questions

1. Open a subject and select **Tạo** (Create) at the top right, or right-click the subject and select **Tạo câu** (Create a question). The Practice page has a **Tạo** button too.
2. Select a **chương** (chapter), then a **bài** (lesson). If it doesn't exist yet, type its name in *Chương mới* (New chapter) or *Bài mới* (New lesson).
3. On the **Tạo** (Create) tab:
   - Choose the question type: **Một đáp án** (single answer), **Nhiều đáp án** (multiple answers), **Đúng / Sai** (true/false), **Điền số** (numeric) or **Điền chữ** (short text).
   - Type the **question**. To add a picture, take a screenshot and paste it into the box with **Ctrl+V**.
   - Type the **options**, then select the letter (A, B, C...) of the correct option to mark it.
   - Type the **solution**, with every step, so others can redo it on their own.
4. Select **Lưu câu vào bài** (Save to lesson). The new question appears in the lesson right away.

![Writing a question in the app](images/4-tao.png)

> For math, write the formula between `\(` and `\)`. For example `\(x^2\)` appears as x². If you're not used to this, use section 4: AI writes the formulas for you.

## 4. Ask AI to write them (easiest)

Works with ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini or any chat AI.

1. Choose the subject, chapter and lesson as in section 3, then select the **Nhờ AI** (Ask AI) tab.
2. Choose a task:
   - **Soạn mới** (Write new): new questions from your slides or exam papers.
   - **Câu tương tự** (Similar questions): more questions like a sample question.
   - **Soát đáp án** (Check answers): ask AI to check the existing questions.
   - **Giải thích** (Explain): ask AI to explain a question you don't understand.
3. Enter the **number of questions** (5–10 is a good amount; AI makes fewer mistakes).
4. Select **Sao chép prompt** (Copy prompt).
5. Open the AI site, **paste** (Ctrl+V), **attach your slides or a photo of the exam** with the site's attach button, and send.
6. The AI replies with a block of text. Select the **Copy** button in the corner of that block.
7. Back in the app, select the **Nhập** (Import) tab, paste into the large box, then select **Nhập vào bài này** (Import into this lesson).

![Ask AI tab: copy the prompt for the AI](images/5-nho-ai.png)

![Import tab: paste the AI's answer and import](images/6-nhap.png)

> **Always try a few questions yourself.** AI sometimes gets answers wrong. If you find one, select **Nghi đáp án sai** (Answer may be wrong), or use **Soát đáp án** (Check answers) to have AI check again.

## 5. Receive a quiz from a friend

Your friend sends you a **`.md`** or **`.zip`** file (by Zalo, Messenger, email...).

1. Download the file.
2. Go to **Practice** and select **Nhập file** (Import file) at the top right of the *Quiz tự soạn và đã nhập* box.
3. Choose the file. Done.

The new quiz appears under **Quiz tự soạn và đã nhập** (Your and imported quizzes), and its questions go into the right subject, chapter and lesson. If your friend sends an updated version, import it again: the app keeps your results.

The app also opens **Moodle XML**, **GIFT** and **Aiken** files (question formats exported from LMS and other quiz sites).

## 6. Send a quiz to a friend

1. Open the subject and select **Xuất** (Export), or right-click the subject or quiz pack and select **Xuất để chia sẻ** (Export to share).
2. Enter **your name** so the recipient knows who wrote it.
3. Select **Lưu Markdown (.md / .zip)** (Save Markdown) and choose where to save.
4. Send that file to your friend. They follow section 5.

![Export tab: save a file to send](images/7-xuat.png)

- If the quiz has pictures, the app saves it as a **`.zip`**; send that file as is.
- **Moodle XML (đưa lên LMS)** (Moodle XML for LMS): for lecturers or teaching assistants who want to put the questions into the LMS question bank.

## 7. Saved LMS quizzes

After each sync, the app saves the LMS quizzes **you have submitted**, so you can review them when LMS is slow or the quiz has closed. Find them in the **Quiz LMS đã lưu** (Saved LMS quizzes) box on the Practice page (the **Kho quiz LMS** page).

- The app **only reads** the review page after you submit. It never reads a quiz in progress, and never takes or submits a quiz for you.
- If a quiz doesn't allow review, the app saves only its name and score. Select **Ghi lại** (Write down) to type the questions you remember; check the answers yourself.
- Saved LMS quizzes **can be sent to others only after the quiz has closed**. The app locks the export button until then.
- To stop saving them, turn off **Settings** > **Sync and reminders** > **Save submitted LMS quizzes for review**.

## 8. If something goes wrong

| Problem | What to do |
|---|---|
| Selecting **Nhập** (Import) shows an error | The app says which line is wrong. For an AI-written quiz, copy the whole error message, paste it to the AI with "fix these errors and return the whole markdown block", then import again. |
| Formulas show strange symbols | Tell the AI: "write formulas inside `\(` and `\)`, don't double the `\`". |
| A question landed in the wrong lesson | The chapter and lesson names in the file must match the names in the app. Asking AI from the right lesson (section 4) fills in the names for you. |
| Delete a question you wrote | Go to **Tạo** (Create) in the right lesson, right-click the question and select **Xóa câu** (Delete question). |
| Remove a whole imported quiz | On the Practice page, right-click the quiz and select **Gỡ quiz này** (Remove this quiz). |

## Appendix: write a quiz file by hand

For people who like Notepad, Word (saved as *Plain Text*), Obsidian or VS Code. Save the file with the `.md` extension, then import it as in section 5. The file's keywords are Vietnamese (for example `mon` = subject, `tac-gia` = author, `Câu` = question) and must stay as they are.

```markdown
---
mon: MT1009 Phương pháp tính
tac-gia: Your name
---

# Chương 1 · Phương trình phi tuyến

## Phương pháp chia đôi

Trung điểm \(x_n=\frac{a_n+b_n}{2}\).

### Câu 1 · Slide tr.36
\(f(x)=x^3-2\) trên \([1,2]\). Theo chia đôi, \(x_2\) là
- [ ] 1.125
- [ ] 1.25
- [x] 1.375
- [ ] 1.5
> \(x_0=1.5\) (dương), \(x_1=1.25\) (âm), nên \(x_2=1.375\).
```

| Write | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `# ...` | chapter name |
| `## ...` | lesson name; the text right below it is the key points |
| `### ...` | a question; after `·` write the source, for example `### Câu 3 · GK251 câu 8` |
| `- [x]` / `- [ ]` | correct / wrong option; several `[x]` make a multiple-answer question |
| `= Đúng` or `= Sai` | true/false question (Đúng = true, Sai = false) |
| `= 0,125 ± 0,001` | numeric question; `±` is the allowed error |
| `= Newton \| Newton-Raphson` | short-text question; accepted answers separated by `\|` |
| `> ...` | solution; every solution line starts with `>` |
| `\( ... \)` | math formula |

- Add `xao-cau: co` and `xao-dap-an: co` to the header to shuffle questions and answers. For a question with an option like "Both A and B", add `· giữ thứ tự` (keep order) at the end of its `###` line.
- For quizzes with pictures, it's simpler to write them in the app (section 3) and export (section 6).
